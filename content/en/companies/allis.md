## Who Allis Electric is

Allis Electric (TW:1514) is a Taiwan heavy-electrical and power-electronics supplier to utilities, semiconductor fabs, telecom, rail, and industrial users. It is not on NVIDIA's published 800 VDC partner list, and the Q1 2026 call did **not** mention 800 V, HVDC, SST, or a DC distribution board. Allis is in this collection as a **facility-power** name: the switchgear and transformers that stand up a fab or an IDC room.

Three plants split the work. **Yangmei** (about 11,000 ping) builds heavy electrical — switchgear, transformers, transmission and distribution gear. **Xinzhuang** (about 3,600 ping) builds power electronics: telecom rectifiers (SMR), UPS, and PV inverters. **Xizhi** builds special-purpose UPS and has seen orders rise with **AI data-center cooling** rooms.

## Business mix

Standing end-markets rather than a single AI line:

- **Taipower and public infrastructure** — GIS, transformers, smart switchgear, STATCOM, rail. Taipower's ten-year grid-resilience programme is a NT$564.5bn backdrop.
- **Semiconductor** — transformers and switchgear into domestic fabs and their overseas sites. This has become the growth mix.
- **IDC / telecom** — substation gear for computer rooms, built on earlier Chunghwa Telecom IDC substation work, plus UPS.
- **Power electronics** — SMR, UPS, PV inverters, and containerised storage.

Named customer types on the call: Taipower, Taoyuan Airport, Taiwan HSR, TRA and MRT; TSMC, UMC and Micron; Chunghwa Telecom, Taiwan Mobile and Far EasTone.

## Where it sits in the NVIDIA 800 VDC stack

**Facility-side AC electrical, not the 800 VDC rack, and not NVIDIA's MV rectifier.**

NVIDIA's conversion layer is an MV rectifier or SST taking 13.8–35 kV AC to 800 VDC, then a **5000 A DC board** and **1500 A busway** into the rack. Allis's disclosed products — MV switchgear, transformers, GIS, STATCOM, UPS, SMR, IDC substations — sit on the **AC side of that conversion**, or as conventional room UPS. The call never mapped them onto a DC board, an 800 V busway, or a rectifier.

A data center still needs that facility plant, and AI halls are clearly pulling orders: management cited about **NT$1bn** of IDC-related bookings from telecom and electronics names. That is substation and room electrical, not a Kyber-rail component. Keep Allis as a second-tier **medium-voltage / facility** read.
