## Who Episil-Precision is

Episil-Precision (TW:3016) is a specialist **epitaxy foundry** — it grows the crystalline layers that power and analog device makers then process, rather than running the device fab itself. Silicon epitaxy is still about **90%** of sales; compound (GaN and SiC) is about **10%**. A third line, germanium-on-silicon for silicon photonics, has just started.

It is not on NVIDIA's published 800 VDC list. It earns a place here because management answered a direct 800V question: **GaN epitaxy is already in 800V HVDC systems**, at small-volume trial.

A major shareholder is **HMC** (漢民科技), with which it is working on 8-inch GaN expansion. Investor conferences are being cut to twice a year.

## Business mix

- **Silicon epitaxy** — MOSFETs, PMICs and other power devices, and the bulk of the P&L. The operational shift is off 4-inch and 5-inch lines (utilisation below 60%) and onto **8-inch**, which went from 55% of silicon-epi revenue a year earlier to **70%** in Q1 2026. 8-inch utilisation is near **90%**.
- **Compound epitaxy** — 6-inch GaN turned profitable in Q1 2026 on AI, humanoid-robot and aerospace demand; 8-inch GaN is qualified and in small production. SiC is growing slowly; a **Japanese IDM** was added, with volume aimed at Q2 2026 for automotive and high-end industrial.
- **Silicon photonics** — germanium/silicon photodetector epitaxy for AI high-speed optics. Qualified at a US customer that has been in the field more than 15 years; small-volume production from February 2026. Gross margin is above the company average; the volume is still small enough that it has not been split out as its own reporting line.

Management's geographic framing is **China for China, non-China for everyone else**. In AI supply chains, customers asking for a non-China epi source is the stated edge against mainland competitors such as Innoscience — on GaN now, and expected on SiC later.

## Where it sits in the NVIDIA 800 VDC stack

One step upstream of the device foundry. Every SiC or GaN switch in an 800 V conversion stage starts as an epi wafer.

The disclosure is unusually plain for this cohort, and equally plain about its limits:

- **GaN is in 800V HVDC systems**, small-volume trial — not a shipping mix number.
- **Silicon MOS is in the AI supply chain**; management does not know whether those parts sit on an 800V rail.
- **SiC** is opaque, because IDM customers will not say what the end application is.

Nine-tenths of the company is still silicon epi for MOSFETs and PMICs, which is the AI *power* recovery, not proof of 800V content. The Ge/Si photodetector line is an AI *optics* story, not a power-architecture one. Keep those three threads separate.
