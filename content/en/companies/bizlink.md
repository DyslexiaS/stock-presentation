## Who BizLink is

BizLink Holding (TW:3665) makes connectors, cable assemblies, and wire harnesses, built up over the last decade through acquisition as much as organic growth. It is one of four Taiwan-listed companies on **NVIDIA's published 800 VDC ecosystem list**, under power system components, alongside Delta, Lite-On and Lead Wealth.

## Business mix

BizLink runs what it calls a 4x4 model — four businesses across four regions (North America, Europe, East Asia, Southeast Asia):

- **Industrial** — the largest segment, spanning factory automation, capital equipment, energy storage, and medical.
- **Automotive** — including EV and charging.
- **IT and datacom** — peripherals plus high-performance computing and data center.
- **Appliance** — the smallest segment.

The stated purpose of that spread is resilience: when consumer-facing demand slows, the industrial and energy businesses are supposed to carry the cycle.

## Where it sits in the NVIDIA 800 VDC stack

The physical interface. NVIDIA's whitepaper is specific about what 800 V requires at the connector: **touch-safe connectors everywhere a human can reach**, **mechanical interlocks** so nothing disconnects under load, and distribution through **1500 A busducts or liquid-cooled cables**. The design approach is borrowed from EV charging, where connectors are already built for untrained consumers at high voltage.

That is BizLink's competence — it already supplies EV charging and industrial high-current interconnect.
