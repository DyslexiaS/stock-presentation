## Who Wiwynn is

Wiwynn (TW:6669) is the Wistron group's listed cloud-server house. It sells racks and data-center systems to hyperscalers, not notebooks. It is not on NVIDIA's published 800 VDC component list. On its own calls it is a **Level-10 to Level-12** assembler: boards, trays and, selectively, full racks.

## Business mix

By FY 2025 the book had split in half:

- **AI servers** — about **50%** of 2025 sales, guided **a little over 50%** in 2026. Inside AI, **ASIC was about 90%** and GPU the rest. Q4 2025 gross margin was hit by a **GB200** GPU wave that then rolled off.
- **General compute** — the other half. 2026 unit shipments guided **+20–30%**, because AI front-end, back-end and some inference still need ordinary servers.

Customers are a small set of large CSPs. Management will not name them on the earnings call. A later AGM (May 2026) discussed North-American concentration; that is not this note.

## Where it sits in the NVIDIA 800 VDC stack

The same integrator slot as Foxconn and Quanta. The February 2026 call never said 800 V or HVDC.

What it did specify:

- New **NVIDIA and AMD GPU** platforms, plus new ASIC platforms, guided to ship in **2H 2026**. NVIDIA is still expected to be the larger GPU contributor; AMD's design was described as **double-wide**, with a first-cut ASP that could approach **twice GB200** (about **US$5m per rack** in that comparison) and a similar gross-margin *rate*.
- **Level-12** (full-rack) demand is **tenders and sovereign AI**, taken case by case.
- **Air- and water-cooling** lines are both in. Factory **power is planned to 2028** across Taiwan, Malaysia, Mexico and the US. The Texas plant started shipping at **end-2025**.
- Consignment on GPU programmes is still being negotiated, with a hope of an answer before the next generation ships.

A 25 May 2026 AGM, after this note, did mention HVDC, CPO and cooling as ecosystem work. Q2 2026 was a **board results release** (7 August), not a 法說會. Do not read those later events back into the February call.
