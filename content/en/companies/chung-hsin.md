## Who Chung-Hsin Electric is

Chung-Hsin Electric and Machinery (TW:1513) is Taiwan's domestic **gas-insulated switchgear** maker, with a claimed **~80%** share of Taipower GIS and **~65%** outside Taipower, plus EPC for hydro, wind and solar. The listed group also runs **DUDU** parking and computer-room HVAC/electrical operations, and builds generators and chillers.

It is not on NVIDIA's published 800 VDC partner list. The April 2026 call did **not** discuss 800 V, HVDC, SST, or DC busway. Chung-Hsin is in this collection because semiconductor fab expansion and **AI / IDC** halls are pulling its **facility** gear — GIS, generators, air-conditioning — not because it is a rack-level 800 V name.

The product line runs **345 kV, 161 kV, 69 kV and 22 kV** GIS. That is utility and campus medium- and high-voltage AC, which sits at the left edge of NVIDIA's diagram (grid 13.8–35 kV AC) **before** any rectifier.

## Business mix

- **Heavy electrical / GIS** — the core, still growing, with Taipower's NT$564.5bn grid-resilience plan plus a further NT$33.4bn distribution upgrade as the domestic backlog engine.
- **Service** — DUDU parking (helped in Q1 after a systems-company acquisition) and IDC-room HVAC/electrical operations, which grow as more halls are built.
- **Generators and air-conditioning** — the line management tied to AI and IDC halls, guided to **+15%** in 2026. Permanent-magnet centrifugal chillers, claimed **30%** more efficient, are already into Chunghwa Telecom, Fubon and Taoyuan Airport, with a Vietnam push.
- **Newer bets** — a shift in hydrogen from delayed buses to a **containerised methanol-to-hydrogen** generating system, with a US R&D base; modular container computer rooms (generator, HVAC, switchgear, server cabinets) under evaluation with listed companies and integrators; overseas GIS via the Haimen, China plant into Japan, India's Kanohar, and Vietnam's ANC (a former Siemens agent).

## Where it sits in the NVIDIA 800 VDC stack

**On the grid and facility AC side, not in the 800 VDC rack.**

NVIDIA converts 13.8–35 kV AC through an MV rectifier or SST into 800 VDC. Chung-Hsin's GIS, transformers-adjacent EPC, generators and chillers electrify and cool the **building**. They do not convert to 800 VDC, do not populate a 5000 A DC board, and were not described as rack power.

The AI data-center print on this call is **generators and HVAC**, plus a possible modular container hall, and a first **semiconductor** GIS order targeted for September 2026 after passing a domestic major's qualification. Those are fab and hall infrastructure orders. Do not read them as Kyber 800 VDC content.
