## Who GW Instek is

Good Will Instrument (TW:2423), sold worldwide under the **GW Instek** brand, is a test-and-measurement house. It also owns Japan's **Texio** and Taiwan's **Prodigit**, which together widen the catalogue to more than 300 instruments. Measurement instruments are **80–90%** of group revenue; environmental testing and video security make up the rest.

It is not on NVIDIA's published 800 VDC partner list. It is in this collection because management said **HVDC-related test equipment**, **solid-state transformers**, and **higher-power PSU modules** are in customer evaluation or development — and that **2027 double-digit growth** depends on those programmes landing.

## Business mix

Five instrument families: power and loads, oscilloscopes, spectrum analysers, signal generators, and safety/measurement gear. Power products are the largest line — about half of sales recently — with measurement instruments and environmental testing the next disclosed slices. Environmental test carries a lower margin because some hosts are sourced from a Japanese partner.

Production is split between **Tucheng, Taiwan** (new high-end products) and **Suzhou** (mature, higher-volume lines). R&D is **8–10%** of revenue, and nearly **30%** of global headcount sits in R&D. Sales run mainly through distributors, which is why management will not give an AI-server revenue share.

## Where it sits in the NVIDIA 800 VDC stack

One layer **before** the hardware ships: the instruments that qualify power supplies, loads, and cooling loops.

NVIDIA's architecture needs high-power PSU and load testing today, and will need HVDC and **SST / MV rectifier** validation if 13.8–35 kV AC is converted straight to 800 VDC. GW Instek's disclosed position splits cleanly:

- **Shipping now:** instruments into **AI servers**, motherboard test, and **air- and liquid-cooling validation** — ASR-6000 and PHU sources, PEL-5000C electronic loads, and data acquisition. This is the current 48 V / high-power-AC generation, not 800 VDC.
- **In evaluation, not in the numbers:** higher-power **PSU modules**, **solid-state transformers**, and **HVDC-related test equipment**. Management's own condition is that **2027 can stay double-digit if those programmes are designed in**.

Treat the HVDC/SST work as a capability in customer labs, not a shipping 800 V line. There is no customer name, no SOP date, and no AI mix figure on this call.
