## Who AcBel is

AcBel Polytech (TW:6282) is a Taiwanese power-supply maker with more than forty years in the trade. The listed company is organised around three groups — power solutions, green-energy services, and electric powertrain — but **power solutions are 95% of sales**. The high-margin telecom line sits mainly in US subsidiary **Omnion**, the former ABB business.

## Business mix

- **Enterprise power** — servers, data centers, and fuel cells. Management puts gross margin in this line at **about 20% or better**. This is also where it parks its AI-related products: data-center PSUs, **CRPS** server power supplies, and fuel cells.
- **Telecom power** — Omnion. Gross margin around **35%**. Management does **not** classify this as AI.
- **Consumer power** — PCs and appliances, gross margin around **10%**. The mix is being shrunk on purpose.
- **New businesses** — green energy, electric powertrain, and metal stamping. A small residual.

## Where it sits in the NVIDIA 800 VDC stack

It does not, on the evidence of the FY2025 call. AcBel never mentioned **800V or HVDC**. The AI print is conventional rack power — CRPS, data-center PSUs, and fuel cells.

Keep it as an adjacent PSU name with a rising enterprise mix. If the screen is NVIDIA 800 VDC disclosure, this is not a hit.

## Strategy

The commercial plan is a mix shift: cut consumer, raise enterprise and telecom, and put the next capex cycle into Taiwan and Omnion against AI-related demand.
