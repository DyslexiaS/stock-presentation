## Who Nidec Chaun-Choung is

Nidec Chaun-Choung Technology (TW:6230), also NCCI, is a thermal house with about **30 years** in heat pipes, vapor chambers and modules. **Nidec owns 86.3%**. Headquarters sit in Sanchong, New Taipei. Plants are in **Kunshan**, **Chongqing** and **Vietnam** (from 2020); Taipei does prototypes. US offices are in San Jose and Austin.

It is not on NVIDIA's published 800 VDC list. The liquid-cooling read is a **group split**, not a full Taiwan loop: NCCI keeps **air cooling**; **liquid-cooling modules** (cold plates) are built at Kunshan; **CDUs and quick disconnects** come from parent **Nidec Thailand**.

## Business mix

By Q2 2025 the mix had already tilted off consoles, but PC was still the base:

- **PC** — **47%** of Q2 2025 sales. Still the operating floor.
- **Servers** — **24%**, up from 16% in 2021.
- **Networking** — **17%**.
- **Game consoles** — **9%**, down from a 26% peak in 2022.

H1 2025 revenue was **NT$4.291bn**, gross margin **17%**, EPS **NT$0.80**. Management blamed soft post-pandemic PC and console demand and said the aim is to get profitability back to **pre-2023** levels.

**Water cooling** was still a new line: guided at **2–5% of 2025** sales and **about 10% in 2026**.

## Where it sits in the NVIDIA 800 VDC stack

The liquid-cooling block: CDU, sidecar, 45°C loop — but only part of that block is on this ticker.

| Part | Who builds it, on this call |
|---|---|
| Air cooling (heat pipe, VC, 3D VC) | NCCI, in volume to brands and ODMs, including Taiwan assemblers |
| Liquid-cooling module / cold plate | Kunshan, jointly developed with Nidec |
| CDU and QDs | Nidec Thailand |
| Manifolds | Still sampling in August 2025; small volume hoped for 2026, then China and Vietnam |

The August 2025 call never said 800 V. What it did say is wattage on the **air** side: cloud-server heat pipes out to **1,000 W**, AI-server **3D VC** for GPUs **above 1,200 W**, switch VC modules to **850 W**, and a roadmap toward **3D VC Fin** at **1,000–1,400 W** by 2028. Intel and AMD **CPU** liquid-cooling modules were already in production. ASIC and PCIe water modules were still in development.

Read Chaun next to Auras and AVC, not as a substitute. Those two already print water as a third or more of sales. Chaun is the listed Nidec vehicle, still mostly air, with the CDU dollar in Thailand.
