## Who TECO is

TECO Electric & Machinery (TW:1504) is a Taiwan industrial group built on motors and electrical equipment, now organised around four groups: electromechanical systems, power and energy, air-conditioning and smart living, and smart energy. Electromechanical plus power and energy are already more than **70%** of sales and rising.

It is not on NVIDIA's published 800 VDC partner list, and the Q2 2026 call did **not** discuss 800 V, HVDC, or solid-state transformers. TECO is in this collection as a **medium-voltage / facility-power** read on data-center buildout — the motors, transformers, switchgear, busway and MEP that electrify a hall.

## Business mix

- **Electromechanical systems** — large and medium-voltage motors, including US demand from data-center cooling customers and oil-and-gas. The largest group.
- **Power and energy** — transformers, switchgear, GIS, busway, and electrical construction. Data-center and offshore-wind work is what has been lifting this group's margin.
- **Air-conditioning and smart living** — mix is being shifted away from residential toward higher-margin **commercial** HVAC and large chillers.
- **Smart energy** — automation and related products, the smallest of the four.

Capacity plans on the call included an Indonesia transformer plant aimed at US orders, a Wuxi HVAC plant for large chillers, and more switchgear/GIS capacity. The **Danasai** acquisition is meant to add prefabricated electrical and Thailand engineering.

## Where it sits in the NVIDIA 800 VDC stack

**Upstream of it, on the AC side of the building.**

NVIDIA's diagram starts at **13.8–35 kV AC**, then an MV rectifier or SST, then a 5000 A DC board and 1500 A busduct into an 800 VDC rack. TECO's disclosed data-center work is the plant that **feeds** that conversion: medium-voltage motors for chillers, transformers, switchgear, facility busway, and MEP. That is real data-center electrical demand. It is **not** an 800 VDC power shelf, BBU, rack, or MV rectifier.

Two traps on this call, both unit confusions:

- **800 kW** is Taiwan's revised **large-user electricity threshold**, expected to pull the regulated population from about 490 sites to about 5,000. It is a kilowatt tariff trigger for energy-service work, **not 800 V**.
- **800–1,000 horsepower** H-pump motors are an oil-and-gas recovery print, **not** an 800 V product.

The Malaysia busway plant's first **data-center** order landed in late July. Management did not say that busway is NVIDIA's 1500 A DC busduct. Do not upgrade it.
