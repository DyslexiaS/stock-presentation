## Who Chang Wah is

Chang Wah Technology (TW:6548) manufactures **leadframes** — the stamped or etched copper-alloy frames that carry a chip's electrical connections and, critically for power devices, its heat. Its global competitors are Mitsui High-tec of Japan and Haesung HDS of Korea.

It is not on NVIDIA's list. It earns a place in this collection because it is the only Taiwan company here with 800V content **already in reported revenue** rather than in a roadmap.

## Business mix

- **Process:** etching is roughly 57% of output and stamping 43%, with etch trending up on power-management ICs and QFN demand. Management expects etch to settle in a 55–60% band.
- **Products:** IC leadframes and QFN are each around a third of sales, with QFP, discrete, and EMC LED making up the rest.
- **Applications:** consumer electronics is the largest bucket, industrial has been rising on data-center power demand, and automotive has been the slower end market.

Customers are IDMs and OSATs rather than end brands.

## Where it sits in the NVIDIA 800 VDC stack

At the packaging step that has to exist before any 800 V rack ships. Every conversion stage in the architecture — the MV rectifier, the power shelf, the 64:1 LLC beside the GPU — steps voltage down, and stepping down produces heat. Copper-alloy leadframes are the thermal path out of those devices.

Chang Wah has disclosed that leadframes for **AI server 800V HVDC** applications are **6–8% of sales** at an **ASP 30–50% above standard products**, requiring special surface treatment, with a target of 10–15% within one to two years. Management has declined to name which conversion stage its parts serve, on the grounds that the systems are still being designed.

## Strategy

A "golden triangle" capacity plan across Taiwan, Weihai in China, and Malaysia, funded while maintaining a stated dividend floor of NT$0.45 per quarter and an average payout above 80% since 2024. The main cost variable is precious metal pricing — gold, silver, and palladium — which is passed through to IDM customers on an index basis.
