## Who Song Chuan is

Song Chuan Precision (TW:7788) designs and makes **relays and contactors**. It was founded in 1974, listed on 16 October 2025, and still sells under its own Song Chuan brand through its own sales force rather than distributors. Six plants — Shulin and Chiayi in Taiwan, Mexico, Xiamen, India, Vietnam — and seven sales offices. Tooling, metal and plastic parts, and the automation equipment itself are designed in house. Automotive-grade quality systems are applied across every line, not only the car parts.

It is not on NVIDIA's list. It is here because it has named **HVDC DC relays**, a **Super Seal** liquid-cooled type, and joint development on next-generation AI PSUs with **Delta, Lite-On and AcBel**, some of it already in qualification.

## Business mix

**New energy was 59% of 2025 sales**, with a target above 70%. Inside that bucket:

- **Energy storage** — portable, residential, commercial and utility cabinets. Named as a core supplier to a US storage OEM, with more than a hundred Song Chuan relays in a single cabinet. Highest margin of the growth lines.
- **Solar inverters and smart grid** — including smart meters.
- **EV and charging** — BMS, OBC, wallbox, supercharger. BMW i3/i8, a Mercedes-Benz new EV platform, and a US EV-maker supercharger programme were named. Margin similar to AI power.

**Power and control** is the AI-data-center line: server PSUs, automatic transfer switches and PDUs.

**Low-voltage automotive** is body control — windows, seats, lights — **40 to 100 relays per car**, on both ICE and EV platforms.

**Appliances** are the legacy line, expected to shrink as a share.

Chiayi and Mexico were expanded together in 2025. Utilisation is held at **80–85%**; Chiayi and Vietnam still have room, and further capacity follows customer programmes. Laboratory rating goes to **2,500 V**.

## Where it sits in the NVIDIA 800 VDC stack

On the **DC switching** path, not in the PSU magnetics and not in the rack sheet metal.

NVIDIA's architecture puts a load-break contactor on the 800 V distribution run, and a high-voltage PSU still needs DC relays inside the box. Song Chuan's disclosed answer is **HVDC DC relays** plus a **Super Seal** fully sealed type aimed at liquid-cooled environments, in development with Taiwan's main PSU houses — Delta, Lite-On, AcBel — with **some programmes in qualification**. That is a component design-in at the PSU makers, not a claim to be on the compute Power Rack.

Keep the other 800 V story separate. Vehicle platforms moving from **400 V to 800 V**, plus V2X and NACS, lift relay content from about US$20–40 on a conventional car to **US$125–250** on an EV. That is automotive high-voltage, not data-center HVDC.

## Strategy

Push new-energy mix above 70%, extend traditional relay know-how into high-voltage, high-current contactors, and use the multi-country footprint when customers want non-China or non-Taiwan origin. Custom work is the ASP lever.
