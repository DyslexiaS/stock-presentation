## Who Jentech is

Jentech Precision Industrial (TW:3653) makes precision parts for **servers — especially AI servers — and electric vehicles**. The pitch is not a single cold plate. It sells a design-plus-process package: lids and vapor chambers at the package, liquid-cooling modules at the system, plus ILM, sockets and backplates, all built in-house from tooling through stamping, plating and assembly.

It is not on NVIDIA's published 800 VDC list. It sits on the cooling half of the same cabinet NVIDIA's 800 V architecture still has to keep at 45°C.

## Business mix

By the first nine months of 2025 the mix had already tilted hard toward thermal:

- **Thermal products** — **73%** of sales, +77% YoY. Inside that line, vapor chambers / lids were +82% and liquid-cooling modules +48%. GPU liquid-cooling for AI servers started to ship in **2025**.
- **Leadframes** — **9%**, −9% YoY. Automotive, LED and 5G. A Japanese competitor exited; orders are moving to Jentech, so 2026 is guided back to growth.
- **Electronic parts** — **7%**, −2%. Server ILM and sockets. Sockets start selling in 2026; the tone is conservative.
- **Other** — **10%**, +49%. Tooling sales and scrap, which move with overall throughput.

## Where it sits in the NVIDIA 800 VDC stack

The liquid-cooling block: CDU, sidecar, 45°C loop — and, in Jentech's case, the **lid that sits on the package** before the loop even starts.

The November 2025 call never said 800 V. What it did say is a ten-year **microchannel liquid-cooling** programme, assembled at the advanced-packaging house, that **removes TIM2**. The claimed point is thermal: simulated and packaged-chip data are said to match, holding a **3,000 W to 7,000 W** die around **70°C**, at about **22 mm** total height (0.5U). Revenue from that programme is **not** in the 2025 outlook scores. Conventional GPU liquid-cooling modules are.

Treat microchannel as a next-generation lid-plus-loop claim, not as 800V evidence. The 2025 P&L is still vapor chambers for advanced packaging and first GPU water-cooling shipments.
