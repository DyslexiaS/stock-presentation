## Who AVC is

Asia Vital Components (TW:3324), traded as 雙鴻, is a thermal house that started in notebook heat pipes and vapor chambers and now sells a **liquid-cooling system**: cold plate, internal manifold, rack manifold, quick disconnects and CDU. Management's own contrast is that in the air-cooling era it was a component maker, and in the liquid era it is a system supplier. It has stayed out of chassis and fans on purpose.

It is not on NVIDIA's published 800 VDC list. Cold-plate and manifold work is on **NVIDIA and AMD recommended-vendor lists**, from the May 2025 call.

## Business mix

By Q3 2025 the mix had flipped toward servers and water:

- **Servers** — **60%** of Q3 sales (water **35%**, air **25%**). Guided to **72%** of 2026 sales.
- **PC** — **27%**, still being tilted toward gaming notebooks.
- **VGA** — **11%**.
- **Other** — **2%**, now mainly automotive after phones were dropped.

Water went from **12%** of 2024 sales to **17%** in Q1 2025, **23%** in Q2 and **35%** in Q3. Full-year 2025 water mix is guided at **35%**, then **above 55%** in 2026. Inside 2025 water revenue: cold plate plus internal manifold **50–55%**, rack manifold **40–45%**, CDU about **5%**.

## Where it sits in the NVIDIA 800 VDC stack

The liquid-cooling block on NVIDIA's diagram: cold plate, manifold, QD, CDU, 45°C loop.

Neither 2025 call said 800 V. The 800V read is the same as Auras: Vera Rubin and GB300 cabinets go liquid, some of them **fanless**, which AVC says makes the **internal manifold larger and more valuable** even if the cold plate itself is under cost pressure. GB300 cold plates and MGX-platform plates were the Q4 2025 volume story. ASIC programmes at existing CSP accounts — some converting from air, some born liquid — are the 2026 growth story.

Self-made content: cold plates and rack manifolds **100%**. In-house QDs are being designed into GB300 and ASIC, with a **~30%** attach target, not a 100% replacement of customer-specified QDs.
