## Who Auras is

Auras Technology (TW:3017) sells thermal and mechanical kits as one package: fans and heatsinks still ship, but the growth is **cold plates, manifolds, quick disconnects, chassis and racks**. It is not on NVIDIA's published 800 VDC list. It sits on the other half of the same cabinet — the 45°C liquid loop NVIDIA specifies next to the new power architecture.

## Business mix

By the first half of 2026 the mix had already flipped toward AI infrastructure:

- **Thermal products** — about **63%** of sales, including liquid-cooling modules.
- **Chassis** — about **20%**, growing faster than thermal as rack density rises.
- **Servers and networking** as an end market — about **66%** of sales, up from the high 40s a year earlier.
- A listed hinge subsidiary remains a smaller slice.

Management's operating rule is profit first, not revenue scale. Q2 2026 revenue was roughly flat sequentially while gross margin jumped.

## Where it sits in the NVIDIA 800 VDC stack

The liquid-cooling block on NVIDIA's diagram: CDU, sidecar, 45°C loop, liquid-cooled busbar.

Auras ships the loop hardware that sits on the tray and in the rack — cold plate, manifold, QD, chassis — and says it can also build CDU internals or a complete water-to-water / water-to-air CDU if the customer defines it as a system. In practice it is investing more in **components** than in whole CDUs.

It is not a named 800V vendor. The 2026 calls never said 800 V. The 800V read is that Vera Rubin, Blackwell and the CSP ASIC cabinets are going fully liquid, and Auras claims a core-supplier seat on both the GPU and ASIC sides. Capacity for liquid-cooling modules is being lifted from **200,000 to 1 million sets a month**, mostly in Vietnam.

For ASIC customers it says it designs the thermal architecture from scratch, not just builds NVIDIA's drawing, and that those programmes carry a higher gross margin than NVIDIA work. 2026 is framed as the **ASIC first year**, with ASIC revenue given a chance to overtake GPU in **2027**.
