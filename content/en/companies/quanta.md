## Who Quanta is

Quanta Computer (TW:2382) is Taiwan's largest notebook contract manufacturer. The live P&L is no longer that company. From Q1 2026, **servers have been more than 80% of sales**, and **AI has been 75–80% of server revenue**. It is not on NVIDIA's published 800 VDC component list. It sits in the same slot as Foxconn and Wiwynn: the listed company that has to ship the cabinet.

## Business mix

By the first half of 2026 the weights were:

- **Servers** — more than **80%** of sales, AI plus general compute. AI is guided to **80%** of that server line in the second half of 2026.
- **Notebooks** — the rest of the book. First-half 2026 shipments **11.5 million** units, better than the prior "flat to slightly up" guide, then guided **down more than 20%** in Q3 and **double-digit down** for the full year as memory and CPU prices squeeze brands.
- **Automotive / smart driving** — still growing double-digit, but a **low-single-digit** mix because AI servers crowd it out.

## Where it sits in the NVIDIA 800 VDC stack

The integrator on NVIDIA's diagram: compute rack, power shelf, 45°C loop. Quanta does not claim an 800 V SKU. The August 2026 call never said 800 V or HVDC.

What it did date is the **factory**. AI-server capacity is guided to **double by end-2026** versus end-2025 (Thailand and California taking more than 80% of first-half capex). The **NT$19.7bn** AUO **Huaya** plant in Guishan is meant for **2028** AI-server backend assembly, next to the R&D HQ and the existing server lines. Management's test-power sentence is that the building already has **high-voltage supply from its panel days**, which it says fits outgoing test of AI servers. That is plant infrastructure, not NVIDIA's single-ended 800 VDC rail.

Customers: large CSPs as the main AI-server book, plus **NeoCloud** GPU and ASIC racks in small volume. Some high-ASP parts move to **consignment** from Q3 2026, project by project — GPU and ASIC both. Visibility is talked about through **2028**.
