NVIDIA is moving AI racks toward **800V high-voltage DC (HVDC)** so that megawatt-class cabinets do not drown in copper. Taiwan is where a lot of the power hardware is designed and built. This hub collects English briefings of those companies' earnings calls — not slide dumps, and not Chinese transcripts.

## What 800V changes

Legacy AI racks sit on 48V/54V or ±400V buses. At 600 kW–1.5 MW per rack, current becomes the bottleneck: thicker busbars, more conversion stages, more heat. 800V HVDC cuts current, which is why NVIDIA's next architectures (including Rubin-class power envelopes) keep showing up in Taiwan calls as **Power Racks**, **BBUs**, **SiC/GaN stages**, and **high-voltage protection**.

Two camps show up in local commentary:

- **NVIDIA path:** 0–800V HVDC racks, often paired with liquid cooling.
- **OCP path:** ±400V racks (ORV3 and related).

Some Taiwan names sell into both. Others are still at prototype.
