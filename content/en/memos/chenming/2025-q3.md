## Key takeaways

- 9M 2025 revenue **NT$7.499bn** (+11%), gross profit **NT$1.31bn** (+26%), GM **17.5%** (from 15.5%), net **NT$501m** (+16%), EPS **NT$2.44**.
- Mix: AI server chassis **47%**, general server 41%, liquid cooling **7%**, desktop 3%, tooling 2%. Liquid cooling due to rise in Q4 2025.
- Thailand phase 1 complete, volume **2026Q1**. Zhongli liquid-cooling line is mature; Taiwan rack production targeted for 2026.
- 2026 growth is framed as **liquid cooling** — sidecar and the power/switch cabinets that spin out of it — contributing from **2H26**.

## NVIDIA 800V / HVDC

Chenming is a chassis/rack OEM (GB200 NVL72 shells, China Superpod, OCP/EIA racks) moving into:

- NVIDIA **MGX** OTS boxes (GB300 1U, NVL4 2U, RTX Pro 4U) — two SKUs through system validation.
- In-house manifold, floating joints, leak-free loops (Taiwan patents).
- **Power Rack + PSU + BBU** samples done with PSU partners.

On architecture, they are explicitly dual-track: **OCP ±400V** and **NVIDIA 0–800V** HVDC both under study. No mix, no SOP for 800V. The near-term money is sidecar / in-rack CDU, not HVDC.

## What it means for the 800V chain

Chenming is the **mechanical envelope** around whatever bus Lite-On and the PSU names put inside the rack. 0–800V vs ±400V is a sheet-metal and busbar problem as much as a silicon problem. This memo is an early 2026/27 option, not a 2025 HVDC print.
