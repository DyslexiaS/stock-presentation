**At a glance.** A chassis and rack OEM whose AI shells are already **47% of sales**, with power-rack samples done and an explicit dual-track: **OCP ±400 V** and **NVIDIA 0–800 V** HVDC both under study. No mix, no SOP for 800V. The near-term money is sidecar and in-rack CDU, not HVDC.

## What changed this year

**AI chassis became the largest line**, at 47% of the mix, including work associated with GB200 NVL72 systems and Chinese superpod deployments.

**Power Rack + PSU + BBU samples were completed** with PSU partners — the mechanical envelope around the power shelf, not the PSU itself.

**Thailand phase 1 was finished**, with volume guided to **2026Q1**. The Zhongli liquid-cooling line is mature; Taiwan rack production is targeted for 2026.

**2026 growth is framed as liquid cooling** — sidecar and the power/switch cabinets that spin out of it — contributing from **2H26**.

## NVIDIA 800V read-through

Chenming is the **mechanical envelope** around whatever bus Lite-On and the PSU names put inside the rack. 0–800 V versus ±400 V is a sheet-metal and busbar problem as much as a silicon problem.

What is in development:

- NVIDIA **MGX** off-the-shelf boxes (GB300 1U, NVL4 2U, RTX Pro 4U) — two SKUs through system validation.
- In-house manifold, floating joints, leak-free loops (Taiwan patents).
- **Power Rack + PSU + BBU** samples done with PSU partners.

On architecture, management is explicitly dual-track: **OCP ±400 V** and **NVIDIA 0–800 V** HVDC both under study. NVIDIA chose single-ended 800 V because two-pole breakers already exist, but OCP's ±400 V rail is real and shipping, and a chassis maker cannot afford to pick wrong.

This memo is an early 2026/27 option, not a 2025 HVDC print. The near-term money is sidecar / in-rack CDU.
