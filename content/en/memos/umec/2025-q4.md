**At a glance.** A December 2025 investor update — Taiwan's equivalent of a US earnings call — covering the first nine months and the 2026 setup, delivered by chairman Ou Jen-chieh. Group revenue was **+21%**. The 800V item is a **switch-side** programme UMEC calls **HBDC**, not HVDC, at **800 V** for **8,000 W and above**. Asked about NVIDIA-style Power Racks, management said it has **no plan to enter compute-server power**.

## What changed this year

**ICP outran the rest of the group.** Information and communications products, mainly Hyundai/Kia millimetre-wave sentinel radar, were **+46%**. Power supplies **+8%**. Magnetics **+1%**. UMEC moved from a Tier-3 radar supplier to an exclusive **Tier 2** on that Hyundai/Kia application in three years.

**Q3 returned to profit** after a Q2 FX loss. Nine-month results were still a loss. The target stated on this call was to get **full-year 2025** back to profit by lifting year-end output.

**HBDC switch power was put on a dated wattage roadmap** — 5.5 kW in 2026, 8 kW in late 2026 or 2027 — and tied to joint development with Accton. Compute Power Rack was ruled out in the same breath.

A year-end **Taichung labour bottleneck** (foreign-worker placement stuck) was the near-term operational risk; a new batch was expected by month-end.

## NVIDIA 800V read-through

Read this as a **switch-PSU 800V development**, not as a compute-rack supplier.

Management's term is **HBDC** (high-voltage DC). The problem it is solving is switch wattage: 2 kW historically, then 4–5 kW, and a path toward **8 kW, 16 kW and higher**. High current is the thermal problem; **800 V** is the proposed answer. On a CRPS roadmap:

- **3 kW** now.
- **5.5 kW in 2026**.
- **8 kW in late 2026 or 2027**.
- Customer sketches at **13 / 16 / 33 kW**.
- Above about **5,000 W**, HBDC is described as required.

**Accton is the named customer.** UMEC was once its sub-150 W supplier; since around 2020 it has been in the high-watt chain, shipping up to **1,200 W**, and is **jointly developing** the next HBDC generation against Accton's system roadmap. That is co-development, not a shipping 800V line and not a date for first revenue.

The hard negative is equally useful. NVIDIA GPU racks may get a separate Power Rack; UMEC said it will **not** chase compute-server PSUs. The iteration speed needs a headcount it does not have. Switch rooms may later adopt a Power Rack-like feed, and after HBDC the **voltage class** starts to resemble the compute hall, but **I/O, cooling and transmission-efficiency** specs remain different. Delta and Lite-On compete in switch power too; UMEC's claim is forty years of joint debug, not a compute qualification.

**"Space data centers"** were the chairman's personal view of when TASA-developed, radiation-hardened DC-DC modules might commercialise — if and when Taiwan system houses enter that market. Treat it as colour, not a schedule. UMEC is not in an international satellite OEM chain.

**Radar.** Hyundai/Kia sentinel: a low-power mmWave sensor that watches a parked car and only then wakes the camera. US attach **above 90%**, exclusive Tier-2 supply, and a possible move from option to standard. Modules ship to a **Vietnam** integrator, so Mexico vehicle tariffs were said not to apply.

**Switch PSU.** Customised enough that noise and power quality are debug items against the customer's switch, not a catalogue brick. 4 kW-class product exists; HBDC is the next architecture with the same customers.

**Drones.** Standard power modules, contact and wireless chargers, mmWave sense for obstacle and ground clearance, sold as a package to **Taiwan** UAV makers that sell into the US. Small revenue contribution guided from **2026**, more after **2027**.

**Lai Ying**, a fibre-laser venture, is still in development with no meaningful sales; its Southern Taiwan Science Park plant is still being built out.

## Management Q&A

**On compute Power Racks and NVIDIA GPUs.** No plan to enter compute-server power. The cycle is too fast and needs a scale UMEC does not have. The company stays on switch PSUs. Switch halls may look more like compute halls on voltage after HBDC, but the PSU specs still differ.

**On HBDC customers and wattage.** Jointly in development with main customers including Accton. CRPS 3 kW now, 5.5 kW in 2026, 8 kW in late 2026 or 2027; 13 / 16 / 33 kW on customer roadmaps. HBDC comes in above about 5 kW.

**On competition with Delta and Lite-On.** Both have switch-PSU lines; UMEC is specialised and says it is not behind on technology. Custom debug is the niche that lets it win some programmes against them.

**On Accton.** Main customer. From sub-150 W to high-watt AI-related supply, now 1,200 W, with HBDC next.

**On automotive mix and Mexico tariffs.** No tariff hit — Vietnam integrator, not Mexico vehicle. Radar about 10–12% of 2025 sales, rising in 2026, still Hyundai/Kia. Attach above 90%; exclusive on this sentinel application.

**On LEO / SpaceX.** Still TASA. Breaking into existing international satellite OEMs is hard. The commercial opening the chairman described is Taiwan system houses entering "space data centers."

**On drones.** Taiwan makers with US-market links, not direct foreign RFQs as the current base.
