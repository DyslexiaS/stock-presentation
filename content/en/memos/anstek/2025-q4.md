## Key takeaways

- FY2025 revenue **NT$8.96bn** (+69%), a record. GM **11.15%** (still >10%, which management calls above-distributor-norm). Operating profit **NT$613m** (+87%).
- EPS only **NT$2.30** because of a Q2 FX hole (non-op loss **NT$424m**). Q3 back to profit; **Q4 EPS ~NT$2**. Cash dividend **NT$1.38** (60% payout).
- Monthly sales already running **NT$0.9–1.0bn** into 2026; Q1 and FY2026 revenue guided to new highs, with FX hedges in place.
- FAE:sales is **1:1**. Lines: AMD/Xilinx FPGA, ADI analog, ITE HDMI, plus new Bourns and SiMa.ai edge AI.

## NVIDIA 800V / HVDC

Anstek does not sell GPUs. The 800V print is **ADI power-management silicon**:

- **800V architecture chips**, including **hot-swap controllers**, have started shipping to **US CSP customers' ODMs**.
- That SKU is called out as the main growth engine inside data-center (data center + storage is **17%** of sales).
- Adjacent: ADI BMS ICs into EV packs and **BBUs**. AMD at Anstek is still mostly embedded SoC, not data-center GPU.

End markets: industrial automation 21%, data center/storage 17%, ATE 15%, multimedia 15%, IPC 13%, defense/aero 11%.

## What it means for the 800V chain

This is a **component-already-shipping** tell, like Chang Wah's 6–8% leadframe mix, and unlike Lite-On's 2027 rack SOP. Hot-swap at 800V has to exist before the rack is production-worthy. Receivables +116% and inventory +90% are the working-capital cost of that growth.
