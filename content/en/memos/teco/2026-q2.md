**At a glance.** A solid industrial quarter — Q2 revenue **NT$16.6bn**, EPS **NT$1.12** — driven by motors and power equipment, including US data-center demand. The 800V-adjacent temptation on this call is a unit trap. **800 kW** is Taiwan's revised **large-user electricity threshold**, not 800 V. TECO did not discuss 800 VDC, HVDC, or SST. What it did discuss is **facility** electrical: large motors, transformers, switchgear, busway and MEP, plus a first data-center **busway** order in late July and Thailand AIDC work via **Danasai**.

## What changed this quarter

**Electromechanical and power-and-energy both printed double-digit growth** and remain more than 70% of the group. Power-and-energy's margin lift is what moved group gross margin.

**The Malaysia busway plant took its first data-center order at the end of July**, with shipments guided to late September–October. A 2.7 MW modular data-center POC in Kuala Lumpur is due to complete around the same window.

**Danasai is closing at the end of August**, with a modular electrical line targeted for September, and Thailand AIDC is being stood up behind a CSP customer that already uses TECO in Malaysia — starting with fibre, not rack power.

## NVIDIA 800V read-through

TECO is a **medium-voltage / facility-power** name. This call is about electrifying data-center **buildings**, not populating an 800 VDC rack.

What is actually in the data-center print:

- **US large motors.** January–July orders up nearly **30%** year on year. One driver is existing large HVAC customers now supplying data centers, which pulls TECO **medium- and high-voltage motors**. The other driver is oil-and-gas: **800–1,000 horsepower** H-pump demand at least doubled. Those are motor horsepower figures, **not volts**.
- **Power equipment into US CSPs** — transformers, switchgear, large motors — plus data-center and offshore-wind contribution inside the power-and-energy group.
- **Malaysia busway.** First data-center application order **late July**, ship late September–October. Management did not identify this as NVIDIA's **1500 A DC busduct**. Read it as facility busway until a voltage is named.
- **Thailand AIDC via Danasai.** Existing Thailand engineering, starting from **fibre**, a dedicated data-center subsidiary, copying the Malaysia playbook, with orders and revenue expected **in 2026**. Follows an international CSP that already takes TECO in Malaysia.
- **2.7 MW MDC POC** in Kuala Lumpur, design done, complete around late September–October, possibly sold rather than only shown.

NVIDIA's stack still needs MV motors, transformers and switchgear on the **AC** side of any rectifier. That is where TECO sits. It is **not** a power shelf, BBU, 800 V rack, or SST name, and it did not claim to be.

**Do not confuse 800 kW with 800 V.** The government revision of Taiwan's large-user rule lowers the threshold to **800 kW**, expanding the regulated set from about **490** sites to about **5,000**. TECO's hook is ESCO work — energy audits, reporting, compressors, chillers — using the same toolkit it already sells to large industrials. Kilowatts, not volts.

**Electromechanical** grew on double-digit China, Taiwan and North America. **Power and energy** grew on data-center and offshore-wind construction; its margin is the group-margin story. **Smart energy** grew on automation. **HVAC** revenue fell because residential was cut back; **commercial HVAC was up more than 20%** in the first half, which is the mix management wants, and large-tonnage chiller capacity is being added at Wuxi.

Other items on the call, not 800V: a first green-power PPA with a semiconductor customer, **>30 MW** and **>300 million kWh**, revenue from end-2026; a Victoria, Australia solar-plus-storage project due to finish installation and testing in **2026Q4**; Indonesia transformer capacity for US orders; TJM-series robot modules shown in July; an India light-commercial e-axle and a mining-truck powertrain prototype; a heavy-lift UAV powertrain in US agricultural and logistics trials.

## Guidance

- **2H 2026 group revenue** expected above the first half.

- **2H gross margin** about in line with the first-half average and with last year.
- **Danasai** consolidation at **end-August**; modular electrical line in **September**; customer visits then orders. Benefit in the second half.

## Management Q&A

**On Thailand AIDC.** Danasai already has Thailand engineering. First cut is fibre; a dedicated data-center subsidiary will copy Malaysia. Hiring and training have started; **2026 orders and revenue** are expected.

**On why Thailand.** Follows an international CSP already using TECO in Malaysia, where delivery and quality were accepted. The customer asked TECO to serve Thailand. Management said Thailand's potential is **no smaller than Malaysia's**.

**On the 800 kW large-user rule.** The applicable population goes from about 490 to about 5,000 sites, which creates energy-audit and equipment-replacement demand. TECO's ESCO offering runs from inventory and filing through optimisation to swapping compressors and chillers. This is a **kilowatt tariff threshold**, not a voltage.

**On HVAC after the mix shift.** First-half revenue fell on residential; the commercial line they are keeping grew more than 20%. B2B is meant to help margin and opex. Large-tonnage chiller capacity is being expanded.

**On the nearly 30% jump in US large-motor orders, and how much is data center.** Two drivers: existing large HVAC customers now supplying data centers, which pulls TECO MV/HV motors; and oil-and-gas recovery, where **800–1,000 hp** H-pump demand at least doubled.

**On Danasai.** Integration on track; **end-August** close and consolidation. Plant tidy-up is done by TECO; a modular electrical line is due in **September**, after which customer visits can release orders. Benefit in the second half.

**On the Malaysia busway plant.** **First data-center order received at the end of July**, shipments from late September to October. The plant uses AI incoming quality control. Non-DC factory demand in Southeast Asia is also described as large.

**On the MDC POC.** 2.7 MW, on plan, design complete, site in Kuala Lumpur, complete around **late September–October**. After that it is a showpiece and possibly a sale.
