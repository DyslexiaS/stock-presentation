## Key takeaways

- Q3 2024 revenue **NT$1.85tn** (+20% YoY, +20% QoQ), net income **NT$49.3bn** (+14%), EPS **NT$3.55** — records for the period on revenue, gross profit, operating profit, and net income.
- Margins: gross **6.19%** (-47 bps YoY) diluted by rack-level AI server mix; operating **2.95%**; net **2.66%**. Nine-month opex ratio fell below **3.5%**.
- AI server revenue more than doubled YoY in the first nine months and was **over 40%** of all server revenue, targeted above **50%** in 2025.
- **GB200 NVL72** called the mainstream spec: production started, small shipments by year-end, volume from **2025Q1**, with a target of holding **40%+** share.
- Mix: consumer **45%**, cloud and networking **32%**, computing **17%**, components **6%**.

## NVIDIA 800V / HVDC

The architecture is not named, but the component list is the giveaway. Asked how it would offset rack-level margin dilution, management listed what it intends to build in-house:

- **Busbars** — the exact item NVIDIA's 800 VDC whitepaper says gets 200 kg lighter per rack when you move off 415 VAC.
- **CDU** (coolant distribution unit), **UQD** (universal quick disconnect), **manifold**, and **cold plate** — all four liquid-cooling parts, already submitted for customer validation.
- NVLink switches, InfiniBand, and Ethernet networking hardware.

Power and cooling in-housing is Foxconn's stated margin strategy, and it is the same parts list that 800 VDC racks require. Management also confirmed AI server rack assembly margins are **high single digit to double digit**, better than branded servers, so the mix is accretive in absolute profit even while diluting percentage gross margin.

## Operations and outlook

Geography is being split by product risk: AI servers concentrated in **North America** (Wisconsin, Texas, Mexico) because of national-security sensitivity, smartphones expanded in **India** from a single-digit share toward **15–20%** within one to two years.

On the semiconductor side, automotive **SiC** was completing customer validation with a Hsinchu SiC module plant in trial production — the same device family that 800 V conversion stages use.

EV: Model B nearing production readiness, battery cell plant pushed to 2025Q1 after typhoon damage, and CDMS talks with two Japanese automakers.

## What it means for the 800V chain

This is the clearest statement of Foxconn's posture: it wants the power and thermal content inside the rack, not just the assembly fee. For Taiwan component vendors that is both the opportunity (Foxconn racks need parts) and the risk (Foxconn intends to make many of them).
