## Why this company matters for 800 VDC

Foxconn was named by foreign brokers among the Taiwan build partners for NVIDIA's **Kyber** rack — the 576-GPU, 1 MW+ system that 800 VDC exists to feed. It is not on NVIDIA's published power-component list; its slot is rack-scale assembly plus the in-house components that rack needs.

## Key takeaways

- Record Q1: revenue **NT$1.6443tn** (+24% YoY), gross profit **NT$100.5bn**, operating profit **NT$46.5bn** (+27%), net income to parent **NT$42.1bn** (+91%), EPS **NT$3.03**.
- Margins: gross **6.11%** (-20 bps YoY, diluted by AI server mix), operating **2.83%** (+5 bps), net **2.56%** (+90 bps).
- Mix: consumer electronics **40%**, cloud and networking **34%**, computing **19%**, components **7%**. AI server revenue grew **more than 50% YoY** and was **over half** of all server revenue.
- Q2 guided to multiply QoQ and YoY in AI servers; full-year outlook raised to significant growth.
- FX matters: each NT$1 of appreciation costs roughly 3% of NT-reported revenue, and TWD had appreciated 8–9%.

## NVIDIA 800V / HVDC

No 800 V discussion on this call. What is here is the platform underneath it: 233 sites in 24 countries, a stated component self-sufficiency target of **50–60%** on products it builds (chips excepted), and over **US$170bn** of annual procurement — larger, management noted, than the roughly US$60bn of the world's biggest EV brand.

That self-sufficiency target is the 800V-relevant number. In a Kyber-class rack the non-GPU bill of materials is busbars, power distribution, cooling, and interconnect. Foxconn's stated strategy is to make those itself rather than buy them, which is how it defends margin against rack-level revenue dilution.

Foxconn also said it would build an advanced compute center in Kaohsiung with NVIDIA around Blackwell GPUs, and sized the AI market at **US$1.3tn by 2032** with roughly US$600bn of that in hardware.

## Operations and outlook

EV is positioned as **CDMS** — design plus manufacturing — with nine prototype vehicles, Model C in volume production, and an international customer committed to a Foxconn platform for Oceania. Smart manufacturing, smart EV, and smart city are being built on the in-house **FoxBrain** LLM.

## What it means for the 800V chain

Foxconn is the integrator that has to physically build whatever power architecture NVIDIA specifies. Watch the component self-sufficiency ratio rather than any 800V headline: that ratio decides how much of the new power content Foxconn captures versus passes to Delta, Lite-On, and BizLink.
