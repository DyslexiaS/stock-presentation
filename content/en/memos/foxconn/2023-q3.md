## Key takeaways

- Q3 2023 revenue **NT$1.5432tn** (+18% QoQ, -12% YoY). All three margins improved: gross **6.66%**, operating **2.99%**, net **2.79%**. Net income to parent **NT$43.1bn** (+11% YoY), EPS **NT$3.11**.
- Margin gains came from mix — CSP and AI server content, plus rising penetration of **titanium phone housings** and camera modules — helped by a weaker TWD.
- Mix: consumer **54%**, cloud and networking **22%**, computing **18%**, components **6%**.
- Full-year 2023 revenue guided flat to slightly down, but gross margin expected to beat 2022's 6.04%. Capex above **NT$90bn**.
- Long-term target restated: **10% gross margin by 2025**, via components, EV, semiconductors, and AI servers.

## NVIDIA 800V / HVDC

No 800 V content — this is a year and a half before the architecture was published. The relevant claims are positional:

- **100% share of GPU modules** and about **50% of GPU baseboards**, the highest-value upstream pieces of an AI server.
- Semiconductor bottlenecks easing, with very strong AI server shipment growth expected in 2024.
- Full-range **1200 V SiC modules** for EV drivetrains launched, plus a first in-house automotive **MCU** test chip and IATF16949 certification.

That 1200 V SiC line is worth noting for the 800V story: NVIDIA's whitepaper explicitly credits the maturity of SiC and GaN devices, and the EV industry's 400 V to 800 V migration, as the reason 800 VDC is feasible now. Foxconn was building EV-grade SiC before it needed data-center-grade SiC.

Management also said AI rack assembly would **not** dilute gross margin, putting it at high single digit to double digit versus mid single digit for branded servers.

## Operations and outlook

Geography by product: India for consumer, Vietnam for computing, the US for cloud and networking, Mexico for EV components, Taiwan and China as R&D centers for EV and semiconductors.

EV: 23 projects in discussion or execution across 14 potential customers; Model C certified in Taiwan with deliveries from January 2024; Foxtron listing on the Innovation Board.

## What it means for the 800V chain

This memo is the baseline for Foxconn's AI position — dominant in GPU modules and baseboards before the rack-power question arrived. Everything in the later memos, including in-housing busbars and cooling, is Foxconn extending outward from that base.
