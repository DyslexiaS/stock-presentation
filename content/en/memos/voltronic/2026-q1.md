## Key takeaways

- Q1 2026 revenue **NT$4.205bn** (-9.3% QoQ, -15.8% YoY). GM **26.1%** (-3.5pp YoY). Net **NT$392m** (-54% YoY), EPS **NT$4.46**.
- Hits were external: Hormuz closure killing Middle East inverter sales, US-China tariff aftershocks, silver/tin/copper/aluminum inflation (**2–3pp** of GM), CNY vs USD (**1–2pp**), plus **NT$320m** FX loss on PRC payables. Effective tax 21% until a PRC high-tech certificate is renewed.
- Online UPS still **41.1%** of sales; offline 29.5%; inverter 18.8% (that last line -38.3% YoY). Asia 55%, Americas 25%, Europe 18%.
- Opex ratio **8.0%**, at the low end of guidance — cost control is not the issue.

## NVIDIA 800V / HVDC

Voltronic is a **DMS** UPS/inverter house (no own brand, 500+ customers, 100 countries). The 800V angle is forward-looking, not in the Q1 P&L:

- High-power **online UPS** outsourcing is accelerating because Tier-1 UPS brands are reallocating engineers to AI.
- A next-gen **HVDC PSU for AI data centers** is through prototype and test.
- Management tied the architecture shift to **NVIDIA Rubin** power: 48V is "not enough," HVDC is "inevitable."
- The technical claim is recycled EV-charger silicon: **400V → 800V → 1000V** already shipped in Supercharger-class products.

No customer, no SOP, no revenue mix. Treat as a **capability print**, not a 2026 earnings driver.

## What it means for the 800V chain

Useful as a second-source / DMS name behind brand UPS vendors, not as a Lite-On peer. If Rubin HVDC racks need a high-voltage PSU that looks like a charger, Voltronic is on the list. If they need a fully custom CSP Power Rack, Lite-On is the name in this hub.
