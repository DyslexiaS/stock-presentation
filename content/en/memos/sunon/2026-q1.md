## Key takeaways

- Q1 2026 revenue **NT$5.02bn** (+2% QoQ, +20.6% YoY), a recent quarterly record. EPS **NT$2.36**. GM **31.7%**, OPM **15.7%**, net margin **12.9%**.
- FY2025 EPS **NT$7.94**; cash dividend **NT$5.5** proposed.
- Mix: networking & servers **60.6%**; IT/notebook 15.6%; industrial/medical 8.8%; auto 8.2%. Server revenue **NT$2.329bn** (47.3% of group), of which AI servers **NT$1.11bn** (22.6%).
- AI-server mix inside that bucket shifted toward GPU (28% in 2025 → **37%** in Q1) and away from ASIC. Q1 AI-server dollars fell QoQ on a customer platform change and parts shortages.
- Q2 described as very strong; 2H better than 1H. Long-term growth pair: **water cooling** plus data-center **EC fans**.

## NVIDIA 800V / HVDC

The 800V claim is product, not mix: a **global-unique 800V EC fan**, 20cm to 90cm+, unit prices into the hundreds of dollars. Management said it has inquiries from **20+ of 34** major infrastructure accounts and expected **first shipments in August**.

That sits beside, not inside, the Q1 AI-server blower/fan P&L. Traditional server fans are 12–54V class. An 800V EC fan is aimed at facility / CRAC / coil-side air that lives on the new HVDC bus, not at a 12V GPU cold plate.

## What it means for the 800V chain

If the August ship date held, Sunon is one of the first **rotating** 800V components rather than a PSU. Treat the "global only" language as marketing until shipments show up in mix. The rest of the print (GPU mix up, AI-server dollars down QoQ) is a platform-transition quarter, not an 800V quarter.
