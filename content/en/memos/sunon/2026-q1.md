**At a glance.** A record quarter on notebooks and networking, but the AI server line actually fell sequentially on a customer platform change. The 800V angle is a single product: a claimed **globally unique 800 V EC fan** for data-center infrastructure, with first shipments guided for **August**.

## What changed this quarter

**AI server revenue fell sequentially** even as the group set a record, on a customer product transition and component shortages. Within the AI mix, **GPU platforms rose from 28% of 2025 to 37%** in Q1, while ASIC platforms fell from 72% to 63%.

**An 800 V EC fan was presented as a first.** Management said it had developed the **world's only 800 V EC fan**, sized from 20 cm to over 90 cm, with unit prices reaching **into the hundreds of US dollars**, and that it had contact and expressions of interest from **more than 20 of the 34 major global infrastructure vendors**, with shipments starting in **August**.

## NVIDIA 800V read-through

Take the claim and the caveat together.

The **800 V EC fan** is a genuine architectural read. NVIDIA's Vera Rubin generation pairs 800 VDC with 45°C liquid cooling and a liquid-cooled busbar, but heat still has to leave the building, and the equipment doing that increasingly sits on the same high-voltage rail as everything else. A fan rated for the new bus is a real requirement, not a marketing exercise.

The caveat is that "only one in the world" is a company claim, and none of it is in the numbers yet. The August ship date is the thing to check. Until then, Sunonwealth's reported business is conventional AI-server thermal — and this quarter that business went sideways.

Management's stated long-term growth pair is **water cooling plus data-center EC fans**, not the legacy notebook and appliance fan business.

## Management Q&A

**Q:** How is the ASIC versus GPU mix of AI-server revenue changing?

**A:** In Q1 2026, GPU was **37%** of AI-server revenue and ASIC **63%**. Versus full-year 2025, when GPU was **28%** and ASIC **72%**, the GPU-related mix is rising.

**Q:** Will next-generation AI-server platforms such as Vera Rubin go fully to water cooling and cut fan demand?

**A:** No. New platforms still include PCIe-architecture designs that remain mainly **air-cooled**, so fan demand continues. Sunon said it is present on those programmes.

**Q:** What is the shipment progress and timetable for cold plates?

**A:** A first batch shipped in Q1 2026. Production lines are due to finish in Q2, **small-lot shipments to brand customers are expected in Q3**, and volumes should build from Q4.

**Q:** What is the outlook for Q2 2026 and the full year?

**A:** Q2 is described as **very optimistic**. For the full year, the second half should be better than the first. The strongest growth engines are servers, networking and power supplies.

**Q:** When management mentioned a materials shortage, was that at Sunon or at customers?

**A:** It is a **long/short-material problem at customers**, especially in mainland China, which led them to pause pull-in. Some orders have been deferred to June, when momentum is expected to recover.

**Q:** What is the 2026 outlook for general servers?

**A:** The market is estimated at single-digit growth, but as a major supplier Sunon sees **growth well beyond single digits**.

**Q:** What will be the main growth driver in 2027?

**A:** **EC fans** will be the key: they go into data-center infrastructure, at high unit prices and with large market potential. Talks are under way with several international vendors; shipments are expected from August this year, with a significant contribution next year.

**Q:** What is the competitive set and unit price for EC fans?

**A:** The main competitors are two German specialists; Sunon says its products can match or even exceed them on performance. These are high-priced parts — a **20 cm** fan is in the **US$100** class, and larger sizes are disproportionately more expensive.

**Q:** What is driving growth in the telecom business?

**A:** The driver is data-center upgrades to **800G and 1.6T switches**. Sunon is offering both air- and water-cooling; the air-cooled solution uses the same class of dual-rotor fans as AI servers.
