**At a glance.** A record quarter on notebooks and networking, but the AI server line actually fell sequentially on a customer platform change. The 800V angle is a single product: a claimed **globally unique 800 V EC fan** for data-center infrastructure, with first shipments guided for **August**.

## What changed this quarter

**AI server revenue fell sequentially** even as the group set a record, on a customer product transition and component shortages. Within the AI mix, **GPU platforms rose from 28% of 2025 to 37%** in Q1, while ASIC platforms fell from 72% to 63%.

**An 800 V EC fan was presented as a first.** Management said it had developed the **world's only 800 V EC fan**, sized from 20 cm to over 90 cm, with unit prices reaching **into the hundreds of US dollars**, and that it had contact and expressions of interest from **more than 20 of the 34 major global infrastructure vendors**, with shipments starting in **August**.

## NVIDIA 800V read-through

Take the claim and the caveat together.

The **800 V EC fan** is a genuine architectural read. NVIDIA's Vera Rubin generation pairs 800 VDC with 45°C liquid cooling and a liquid-cooled busbar, but heat still has to leave the building, and the equipment doing that increasingly sits on the same high-voltage rail as everything else. A fan rated for the new bus is a real requirement, not a marketing exercise.

The caveat is that "only one in the world" is a company claim, and none of it is in the numbers yet. The August ship date is the thing to check. Until then, Sunonwealth's reported business is conventional AI-server thermal — and this quarter that business went sideways.

Management's stated long-term growth pair is **water cooling plus data-center EC fans**, not the legacy notebook and appliance fan business.
