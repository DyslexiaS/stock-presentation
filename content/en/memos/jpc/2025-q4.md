## Key takeaways

- This call was a **product/certification** update, not a numbered earnings print.
- Optical: spec-in has become spec-win on telecom transport (from 2025Q1). 1.6T loopback certified at a US switch OEM in Q4. 1.6T ACC samples due 2026Q1. Silicon photonics team plus TW/EU patents; 2026Q1 module samples with a US AI-chip name.
- Power: OCP **ORV3 AC whip** got UL and a production order in **2025Q4**, first revenue **2026Q1**.
- Memory SoCam connector through performance validation; automation/cleanroom ready in 2H25.

## NVIDIA 800V / HVDC

JPC's HVDC work is **busbar**, not 800V PSU:

- Customers moving from **48V/54V** toward **±400V HVDC**.
- Orders pursued through system-integrator "hubs," not named CSPs.
- That is the **OCP-style** rail, not NVIDIA's 0–800V rack. It still sits in the same "get copper off the 54V bus" trade.

Trident cable into a US compute-tray supply chain is the other high-density interconnect print.

## What it means for the 800V chain

Keep JPC in the hub as a **±400V / ORV3** name, and do not flatten it into NVIDIA 800V. The UL AC-whip order is the 2026 near-term. The busbar is a follow-on. If you only want NVIDIA-rail names, Lite-On / Chenming / Chang Wah are cleaner.
