## NVIDIA 800V / HVDC

Management's architecture line is unusually direct: rack power has gone from **480V AC to 800V HVDC**. Polytronics' answer is not an 800V fuse on the DC bus (they did not claim that) but **277V and 305V AC PPTC** parts plus **SiC** power devices for the new front end. Named accounts: **Amazon**, **Super Micro**.

EV is a separate 48V/BMS story (Tesla, Ford, Hyundai, Kia after a 15-year 48V high-temp program). Automation names (ASML, Applied, Bosch) are a decade-plus socket, not 800V.

## What it means for the 800V chain

This is **circuit protection** on the HVDC/AC boundary, shipping into US cloud ODMs. Combine with Anstek's ADI hot-swap IC and Chang Wah's HVDC leadframes and you get a 2026 *component* stack, while Lite-On's rack is still a 2027 volume SKU. That lag is the investment map: BOM first, rack later.
