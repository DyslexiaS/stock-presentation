## Key takeaways

- Q1 2026 revenue **NT$3.672bn** (+5% QoQ, +15% YoY), GM **21.7%**, OPM **13.8%**, net to parent **NT$469m**, EPS **NT$0.51**.
- Etch mix **56%** / stamp 44%. IC leadframes 32%, QFN 30%, discrete/SOT 18%, QFP 11%, EMC LED 9%.
- Industrial mix rose to **26%** on data-center power-management; 3C 47%; auto 24% still sleepy.
- April revenue "easily" a record; Q2 guided to **double-digit** growth. Malaysia and China expansions underway.
- Inventory **NT$3.558bn** (+NT$630m QoQ) on both volume prep and metal prices.

## NVIDIA 800V / HVDC

First hard mix number in the series (later confirmed on the July call):

- **800V HVDC** packages for IDMs: **6–8% of total sales**.
- Special surface treatment required.
- ASP **30–50%** above standard.
- Q&A: yes, the leadframe needs a process upgrade for 800V HVDC, not just a catalog part.

Power-management IC leadframes were already up double digits QoQ and YoY in absolute dollars. Discrete SOT also bounced with data-center power.

## What it means for the 800V chain

By April 2026, 800V was not a 2027 footnote at Chang Wah. It was a mid-single-digit, high-ASP slice of a listed leadframe company. That is earlier than Lite-On's rack SOP, which is the usual pattern: packaging and protection qualify before the rack hits volume.
