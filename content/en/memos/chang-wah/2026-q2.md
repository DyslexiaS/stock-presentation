## Key takeaways

- Q2 2026 revenue **NT$4.32bn** (+18% QoQ, +36% YoY). Net profit to parent **NT$660m** (+40% QoQ, more than 5x YoY). EPS **NT$0.71**. Margins up on all three lines.
- Growth was broad: QFN, QFP, IC leadframes. Power-management was the strongest application. Industrial + auto MCU restocking lifted QFP to **13%** of sales.
- EV, data center, LEO, and humanoid combined **~25%** of revenue.
- Q3 guided above Q2 on both QoQ and YoY.

## NVIDIA 800V / HVDC

This is the rare 800V print that is already in the P&L, not a 2027 SOP:

- Leadframes used in **AI-server 800V HVDC** were **6–8% of sales**.
- ASP **30–50%** above standard leadframes.
- Management wants that mix at **10–15%** within one to two years.

Etch was **57%** of Q2 process mix (stamp 43%), pulled by power-management ICs and QFN. Mix is expected to stay in a 55–60% etch band. IC leadframes and QFN were each **32%** of product sales; discrete 15%; EMC 8%.

Application mix: 3C ~46%, industrial **28%** (power-management + AI/data-center spillover into automation, robots, LEO), auto still slower.

## What it means for the 800V chain

Chang Wah is a **materials/packaging** tell, not a rack tell. If 800V HVDC were vapor, 6–8% of a listed leadframe book would not be this explicit. The 10–15% target is the 2027/28 bull case; the 30–50% ASP gap is why the mix matters more than the headline 6%.

Peers in this hub (Lite-On racks, Polytronics PPTC) sit further downstream. Chang Wah sits at the IDM/OSAT leadframe step that has to exist before those racks ship.
