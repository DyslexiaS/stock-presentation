## NVIDIA 800V / HVDC

Q&A asked which conversion stage Chang Wah owns inside 800V HVDC. Answer: they will not name a stage while systems are still being designed. The pitch is physical:

- Whatever the bus voltage, you still step down, and step-down dumps heat.
- Copper-alloy leadframes are the thermal path.
- Higher system ASP (AI servers) raises the reliability bar, which favors qualified suppliers.

GaN power ICs in QFN are the adjacent high-efficiency package story. MiniLED EMC leadframes (auto, >50% GM, ~US$2.5–3.0m/month) are a separate high-margin pocket.

## What it means for the 800V chain

Use this memo as the **qualitative** 800V note and the April/July prints as the **mix** notes. Management is present on 800V but refuses a block-diagram seat. That is consistent with a leadframe vendor selling into several conversion stages at once.
