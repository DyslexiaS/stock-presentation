**At a glance.** This is the most recent BizLink call in the archive, from **December 2023** — well before NVIDIA published the 800 VDC architecture. It contains **no 800 V content**. It is here because BizLink is one of four Taiwan-listed names NVIDIA later put on its power-component list, and this is the only briefing we have.

## What changed this quarter

**IT and datacom was the problem.** A major customer cut its outlook; the segment fell **28%** year on year to 20% of sales. Management still pointed to HPC and data center as the recovery path, with new products launching in Q4 2023.

**The balance sheet turned.** Four consecutive quarters of positive free cash flow; cash exceeded debt by Q3, with deleveraging the priority.

**A one-off refinancing cost hit the quarter.** A **US$3.5m** syndicated-loan fee write-off after a successful refinancing.

Opex held near **NT$2.1bn** for five consecutive quarters.

## NVIDIA 800V read-through

There is none in this call. That is the fact, not a gap in the briefing.

BizLink's official slot is physical: NVIDIA's whitepaper calls for **touch-safe connectors with mechanical interlocks** so nothing disconnects under load, plus **liquid-cooled cables** and 1500 A busducts. The connector approach is borrowed from EV charging, which is already BizLink's competence.

Two things to take from the 2023 call anyway:

- The **HPC and data-center product cycle** management said was starting in Q4 2023 is the commercial root of the position NVIDIA later recognized.
- This is a **coverage gap**, not a coverage answer. Until a current call is in the archive, treat the official listing as the fact and this memo as background.

## Segments that moved

BizLink runs a "4x4" model — four businesses across North America, Europe, East Asia, and Southeast Asia.

| Segment | Share of sales | YoY |
|---|---|---|
| Industrial | 40% | −3% |
| Automotive | 24% | +7% |
| IT and datacom | 20% | −28% |
| Appliance | 15% | −7% |

**Industrial** was the anchor: factory automation cautious on soft global PMI, capital equipment recovering (+12% QoQ in Q3), and energy strong on industrial storage.

**IT and datacom** was the trough: a major customer cut its outlook, expected around mid-2024. Management was nonetheless positive on HPC and data center, said the downstream supply chain had begun to recover, and flagged new products in Q4 2023 expected to contribute through 2024.

**Automotive** was cautious on EV under high rates, with non-EV and charging more constructive.

## Guidance

No numbered outlook. The qualitative message was deleveraging, a mid-2024 trough in IT and datacom, and new HPC/data-center products starting to contribute from Q4 2023 into 2024.
