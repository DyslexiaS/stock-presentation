## Why this company matters for 800 VDC

BizLink is one of three Taiwan-listed names on NVIDIA's published 800 VDC ecosystem list, under power system components. Its slot is physical: NVIDIA's whitepaper calls for **touch-safe connectors with mechanical interlocks** so nothing disconnects under load, plus **liquid-cooled cables** and 1500 A busducts. That connector approach is borrowed from EV charging, which is BizLink's existing competence.

**Read the data caveat first.** The call summarized below is from December 2023 — well before NVIDIA published the 800 VDC architecture. It contains no 800 V content. It is here because BizLink has an official slot and this is the most recent call in our archive; the memo documents the company, not its current 800V position.

## Key takeaways

- Q3 2023 revenue **NT$13.15bn**, flat QoQ and down **9%** from the prior-year peak. Gross margin **26.69%**, operating margin **10.75%**, net income **NT$740m**, EPS **NT$4.54** (+65% QoQ, -40% YoY).
- Excluding a **NT$683m** inventory adjustment, gross margin was **26.55%** versus 24.70% reported for the nine months — management's point being that underlying margin had been recovering for several quarters.
- Opex held near **NT$2.1bn** for five consecutive quarters. Four consecutive quarters of positive free cash flow; cash exceeded debt by Q3, with deleveraging prioritized.
- A one-off **US$3.5m** syndicated-loan fee write-off hit non-operating expense after a successful refinancing.

## Segments

BizLink runs a "4x4" model — four businesses across North America, Europe, East Asia, and Southeast Asia.

| Segment | Share of sales | YoY |
|---|---|---|
| Industrial | 40% | -3% |
| Automotive | 24% | +7% |
| IT and datacom | 20% | -28% |
| Appliance | 15% | -7% |

- **Industrial** was the anchor: factory automation cautious on soft global PMI, capital equipment recovering (+12% QoQ in Q3), and energy strong on industrial storage.
- **IT and datacom** was the problem: a major customer cut its outlook, with the trough expected around mid-2024. Management was nonetheless positive on **HPC and data center**, said the downstream supply chain had begun to recover, and flagged **new products launched in Q4 2023** expected to contribute revenue and margin through 2024.
- **Automotive** was cautious on EV under high rates, with non-EV and charging more constructive.

## What it means for the 800V chain

Two things to take from this.

First, the HPC and data-center product cycle BizLink described starting in Q4 2023 is the commercial root of the position NVIDIA later recognized. The company was rebuilding that segment right as AI rack power began to change.

Second, this is a coverage gap, not a coverage answer. To write a current BizLink 800V memo we need a recent earnings call in the archive. Until then, treat BizLink's official listing as the fact and this memo as background.
