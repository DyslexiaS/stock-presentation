## Key takeaways

- FY2025 revenue **NT$166.1bn** (+21%), GM **22.9%**, OPM **10.1%**, net income **NT$15.1bn** (+27%), EPS **NT$6.64**.
- Cloud & IoT reached **45%** of 2025 sales and grew more than 70% YoY. High-value mix (cloud + opto) went from 51% in 2021 to **62%** in 2025.
- AI-related revenue was **>20%** in 2025; 2026 target **>30%**.
- 2026Q1 guided for QoQ and YoY growth in core businesses. 2026 capex guided to **NT$11bn** (from ~NT$7bn).
- Acquisition of **U-Connect / 宇智網通** to bolt on 5G / AI-RAN / FWA.

## NVIDIA 800V / HVDC

This is the first call in the series that puts 800V on a factory calendar:

- **800V DC power cabinet** prototypes in test; **small-volume 2026Q4**, **volume 2027Q1**. Prototype validation was due to finish in **2026Q3**.
- **800V BBU** planned for 2026, with a different cell and design than the 50V generation. BBU capacity added last year was already sold out.
- What *is* shipping in 2026Q1: **±400V / 50V DC Power Rack** to a lead customer. Management said the market is rotating from that rail toward 800V HVDC.
- GTC would show a next-gen **800V DC megawatt rack plus liquid cooling**.

33kW Power Shelf and BBU were the 2025 workhorses; **110kW Power Shelf** plus HVDC cabinets are the 2026/27 step-up.

## Operations and outlook

Q4 2025: revenue NT$44.4bn (+16% YoY, -1% QoQ), GM 21.7%, EPS NT$1.70. QoQ softness was blamed on DRAM supply hurting non-cloud pull-ins. Q3's 25% GM was flattered by tariff accounting; normalized closer to 23%. R&D was 5.3% of 2025 sales.

IT & consumer (38% of 2025) is being pushed up-stack (industrial and LEO PSUs; >50% share in satellite power). Opto (17%) is mixed: consumer weak, AI/industrial IR and miniature visible-light parts better.

## What it means for the 800V chain

By Feb 2026 Lite-On had already told the market that 800V is a **2027 volume** product, with 2026 reserved for samples and 400V racks. Later calls only moved the sample date around inside 2H26. That is useful: 800V is not "already in the P&L."
