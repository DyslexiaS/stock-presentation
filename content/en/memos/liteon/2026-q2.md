## 1. Lite-On operating summary

Lite-On (TW:2301) reported record results for Q2 2026, driven by demand for AI server power and energy storage.

- **Record quarter.** Revenue **NT$52.7bn** (+30% YoY, +21% QoQ). Gross margin **27.2%**, operating margin **15.6%**. Net income **NT$7.1bn** (+126% YoY) and EPS **NT$3.14**, both records. First-half EPS **NT$4.8** (+66%).
- **Cloud is now the company.** Cloud and IoT was **55%** of Q2 sales, with cloud computing growing more than 70% YoY. Management targets **AI-related revenue above 30%** of the full-year mix.
- **Capacity expansion.** 2026 capex was raised to **NT$18bn**. Kaohsiung phase 2 starts in Q3 and Vietnam Quang Ninh in Q4. Non-China capacity is targeted at **70% by 2027**.
- **Strategic investment.** The board approved a **21%** stake in Singapore photonics company Denselight, to secure optical engine and module technology for AI data centers.
- **Outlook.** Management called 2H 2026 very optimistic, guiding cloud computing to grow **over 70%** for the year with full-year gross margin around **25%**.

## 2. Lite-On business and product mix

Lite-On has moved from ODM/OEM work toward high-value solutions built around AI infrastructure: power management, thermal, and high-speed optical.

- **Cloud and IoT.** The main engine, **55%** of Q2 sales versus 32% two years earlier. Products include AI server **PSUs**, **BBUs**, **power shelves**, and **rack-level solutions**. Liquid-cooling products including a **120kW CDU** and **Sidecar** entered volume production in Q2 2026.
- **Optoelectronics.** Beyond legacy couplers, the Denselight investment targets next-generation high-speed optics. Combining Denselight's indium phosphide (InP) epitaxy and chip capability with Lite-On's advanced packaging aims at **1.6T/3.2T optical engines, modules, and CPO**.
- **IT and consumer electronics.** End demand is soft and possibly worse than expected. Lite-On is raising prices to offset cost inflation and volume decline, and expects 2H to be flat versus 1H.

## 4. Lite-On market and product developments

- **Market.** AI is driving demand for high-power supply, efficient cooling, and high-speed transmission. Lite-On has entered every major North American CSP and has begun shipping power and rack products to **two new CSP customers**.
- **Power management.** Next-generation **110kW Power Shelf**, **BBU**, and **800V HVDC Power Rack** enter qualification and production through 2H 2026. The HVDC rack is due for **small-volume production in November 2026** and **volume in 2027Q1**.
- **Liquid cooling.** The **120kW CDU** and **Sidecar** entered volume production in Q2 2026 and ramp through 2H.
- **Rack solutions.** Rack-level cabinets have been qualified and ordered by North American CSPs.
- **DC-DC.** The acquisition of Nanjing Nengli Xin positions Lite-On in high-end DC-DC bricks to match future HVDC architectures.
- **Strategic partnership.** The **21%** Denselight stake is vertical integration: a Singapore IDM with full InP epitaxy and process capability, core to Lite-On's future CPO and optical engine roadmap.

## 5. Lite-On strategy and future development

- **Technology.** R&D is held at roughly **5–5.5%** of revenue, concentrated in power, storage, thermal, and high-speed optics.
- **Global footprint.** Manufacturing across Taiwan, Vietnam, Thailand, and North America. Non-China capacity should exceed **60% by end-2026** and reach **70% in 2027**.
- **Transformation.** The shift from contract manufacturing to high-value solutions has lifted return on invested capital from 28% in 2020 to 55% in 2023.
- **Competitive position.** Design-in at all major North American CSPs, leadership in high-power AI supply and liquid cooling, and an early position in next-generation optics through Denselight.

## 6. Lite-On outlook and guidance

- **Revenue.** 2H 2026 described as very optimistic. Cloud computing revenue guided to grow **over 70%** for the year, with AI-related products above **30%** of the mix.
- **Margin.** Despite the 27.2% print in Q2, management said the **24.7%** first-half average is the sounder reference, with a full-year target of **25%**.
- **Capex.** Raised to **NT$18bn**, with capex in following years not expected to exceed this level.
- **Growth.** New CSP orders ramp gradually, with a long-term target of **US$1bn** of annual revenue per major customer. 800V HVDC, liquid cooling, and optics are the next wave.

## 7. Lite-On Q&A highlights

**Q: Why did Q2 gross margin jump to 27.2%, and is it sustainable?**
Two reasons: some high-end cloud products slipped from Q1 into Q2, lifting average margin; and Q1 carried an inventory write-down that depressed the base. The **24.7%** first-half figure is the sounder reference, with a **25%** full-year target. Material price risk remains in 2H.

**Q: What is the raised capex for, and does it affect dividends?**
The increase from NT$13bn to NT$18bn includes a Texas plant. Roughly **30%** goes to land and buildings and **70%** to production, R&D, and automation equipment, particularly high-power test rigs. This is the peak year; later years should not exceed it.

**Q: How are you handling component shortages and price increases in 2H?**
Strategic stocking was done in Q1 and Q2 with customer coordination, including subsidies or risk guarantees. Critical components for 2H are secured, so shortages should not block growth, and cost increases are largely passed through.

**Q: Progress with new CSP customers?**
Shipments have started to **two additional North American CSPs**, covering power supplies for ASIC and GPU platforms plus racks. Volumes are small but next-generation development is underway. The long-term goal is **US$1bn** per major customer, potentially within two years.

**Q: Why invest in Denselight?**
Three reasons: technology integration of its InP chip capability with Lite-On's packaging for 1.6T/3.2T engines and CPO; a supply chain fully outside China; and strong AI optical demand. Revenue contribution is expected around **2027–2028**.

**Q: What is the 800V HVDC Power Rack schedule?**
Sample testing in **August 2026** with roughly a 13-week cycle. If it goes well, **small-volume production in November 2026** and **larger volume in 2027Q1**. More than one customer is pushing this specification; Lite-On is working closely with the one furthest along.

**Q: The 2H view on IT and consumer electronics?**
Unchanged and possibly worse than expected. Lite-On is less exposed because it has raised prices to offset volume, so 2H revenue in that segment should be flat.

**Q: Cloud growth for the year, and BBU within it?**
The **over 70%** cloud growth target is intact, with first-half growth close to double. BBU tracks the segment and is roughly **20–25%** of cloud revenue. As HVDC power racks become mainstream, BBU may become standard equipment, raising volumes further.
