## Key takeaways

- **Record quarter.** Lite-On (TW:2301) reported Q2 2026 revenue of **NT$52.7bn** (+30% YoY, +21% QoQ), gross margin **27.2%**, operating margin **15.6%**, net income **NT$7.1bn** (+126% YoY) and EPS **NT$3.14**. First-half EPS was **NT$4.8** (+66%).
- **Cloud is now the company.** Cloud & IoT was **55%** of Q2 sales, versus 32% two years earlier. Management still targets **AI-related revenue above 30%** of the full-year mix, and **>70%** growth in cloud computing for 2026.
- **800V has a date.** Next-gen **110kW Power Shelf**, **BBU**, and **800V HVDC Power Rack** enter qualification in 2H26. The HVDC rack is due for **small-volume production in November 2026** and **volume in 2027Q1**.
- **Capacity is chasing the same cycle.** 2026 capex was raised to **NT$18bn**. Kaohsiung phase 2 (Q3) and Vietnam Quang Ninh (Q4) are the near-term adds. Non-China capacity is targeted at **70% by 2027**.
- **Optics option.** The board took a **21%** stake in Singapore photonics name Denselight (InP epi/chips) for 1.6T/3.2T engines and CPO — a second AI-data-center bet besides power.

## NVIDIA 800V / HVDC

This is the cleanest dated 800V print in the Taiwan power cohort.

Management said 800V HVDC, liquid cooling, and optics are the *next* growth layer after the current 50V / ±400V rack wave. A **DCDC brick** push (via the Nanjing Nengli Xin deal) is meant to sit inside future HVDC architectures. On the Q&A, BBU is already **20–25%** of cloud revenue; once HVDC racks become standard, BBU is expected to ship as default kit rather than an option.

Read-through for the chain: Lite-On is not waiting for 2028. Sampling this November implies BOM partners (leadframes, fans, busbars, protection) need to be qualified on a 2026/27 clock.

Liquid cooling is already shipping: **120kW CDU** and **Sidecar** started volume in Q2 2026.

## Operations and outlook

IT & consumer remains the drag; management will hold price rather than chase volume and expects 2H flattish versus 1H in that segment. Photoelectronics is being re-aimed at high-speed optical via Denselight rather than legacy couplers.

H1 2026: revenue **NT$96.1bn** (+25%), GM **24.7%**, OPM **12.8%**, net **NT$10.9bn**. 2H cloud commentary was "very optimistic," with full-year GM guided around **25%**.

## What it means for the 800V chain

Lite-On is the **rack-level integrator** in this collection: Power Shelf + BBU + HVDC rack + CDU. If November sampling slips, the whole Taiwan 800V narrative slips with it. If it holds, names that already print 800V mix (Chang Wah leadframes, Sunon 800V EC fans, Polytronics high-voltage PPTC) get a 2027 volume story rather than a slide-deck story.
