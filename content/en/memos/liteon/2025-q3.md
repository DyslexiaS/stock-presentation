## Key takeaways

- Q3 2025 revenue **NT$44.9bn** (+22% YoY, +11% QoQ), GM **25%**, OPM **10.4%**, net **NT$4.65bn**, EPS **NT$2.05** (+47% QoQ). 9M EPS **NT$4.94** (+27%).
- Cloud & IoT **48%** of Q3 sales, +28% QoQ. High-end AI power SKUs were up nearly 40%. Full-year 2025 AI mix guided to **~20%**, then higher in 2026.
- Vietnam subsidiary to be recapitalized by **US$200m**. Domestic unsecured CB of up to **NT$12bn** approved.
- Q4 core business guided for both YoY and QoQ growth.

## NVIDIA 800V / HVDC

At **OCP 2025** Lite-On showed a megawatt-class power + liquid-cooling stack:

- **800V DC Power Rack**
- BBU
- **2.1MW in-row CDU**
- **280kW in-rack CDU**
- **140kW liquid-to-air sidecar**

The dated split on Q&A was blunt: **400V Power Rack** samples in **2025Q4**, volume in **2026**; **800V Power Rack** tied to a customer **Xyber** platform, volume in **2027**. That is the earliest explicit "800V = 2027" print in this set.

## Operations and outlook

9M 2025: revenue NT$121.7bn (+23%), GM 23.3%, net NT$11.3bn. High-value mix (cloud + opto) **62%** of 9M sales vs 51% in 2021. IT & consumer was 36% of Q3, helped by high-end IT, LEO, and smart-input devices. Opto 16%.

## What it means for the 800V chain

October 2025: 800V is a **show-floor and 2027 SOP** item. 400V is the 2026 money. Anyone modeling Lite-On 2026 as an 800V year was early — and management said so.
