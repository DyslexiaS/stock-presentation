## Key takeaways

- Q1 2026 revenue **NT$43.4bn** (+19% YoY). Reported GM **21.7%** / OPM **9.4%**; ex a one-off inventory charge, **22.8% / 10.5%**. Net income **NT$3.78bn** (+10%), EPS **NT$1.66** — a five-year Q1 high.
- Cloud & IoT was **53%** of sales (31% in 2022Q1). That segment was up nearly 50% YoY in revenue and **60%** in operating profit.
- Capex (ex-US) guided to **NT$13bn** vs NT$7bn last year. Board also approved **US$919m** of US investment for AI energy products and **US$149m** into the Vietnam unit.
- AI-related revenue still targeted near **30%** of 2026 sales. 110kW Power Shelf and **800VDC Power Rack** were named as the 2H growth engines.

## NVIDIA 800V / HVDC

The 800V print on this call is more specific on *power density* than on calendar:

- A hyperscaler **800VDC Power Rack above 1.5MW per cabinet** was pulled forward: validation targeted for **Q3**, production for **Q4**.
- A **2.5kW DC-DC power brick** was also due to enter production.
- Management positioned Lite-On as already in custom 800VDC work with major cloud customers, not as a 2028 science project.

That Q4 production comment is *earlier* than the November-2026 / 2027Q1 timing given on the July call. Treat the two prints as a tightening window, not a contradiction to overfit: qualification with one hyperscaler can start in 4Q26 while broader HVDC rack volume still sits in 2027Q1.

## Operations and outlook

Q2 was guided above Q1 on both revenue and profit, led by AI power-management systems and LEO-satellite PSUs. Photoelectronics was 17% of sales; IT & consumer 30%, with satellite power offsetting a weak PC/console mix. Inventory was built into Q2 demand.

## What it means for the 800V chain

The US$919m US plant is the geopolitical tell: Lite-On wants HVDC/AI power assembled onshore for US CSPs. Taiwan still does the design and a large share of the BOM. Watch whether 4Q26 "production" is a handful of racks or a real line.
