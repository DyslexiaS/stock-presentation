## Key takeaways

- Q2 2025 revenue **NT$40.4bn** (+21% YoY, +11% QoQ), GM **22.1%**, OPM **9.2%**, net **NT$3.16bn**, EPS **NT$1.39**. H1 revenue **NT$76.8bn** (+24%), EPS **NT$2.89**.
- All three groups grew YoY and QoQ. Cloud & IoT was **NT$16.7bn** (41% of sales, +37% YoY); AI server power and energy-management SKUs inside that group were up **~50%**.
- TWD strength cost about **4%** of revenue and 0.2/0.5pp of GM/OPM, per the CEO. Prepaid US BBU tariffs of just over **NT$400m** inflated opex.
- Cash dividend NT$2 for Q2. Treasury stock 24,219 lots to be cancelled.

## NVIDIA 800V / HVDC

800V is only a **Computex demo** on this call: HVDC plus SiC/GaN efficiency kits "to help customers build energy-saving AI data centers." No SOP date, no customer, no MW rating. The money in Q2 2025 is still conventional AI PSU / BBU / energy-management, not 800V racks.

That makes this memo the baseline: six months later the same company is quoting November 2026 sampling. The 800V story accelerated in the 2H25–1H26 calls.

## Operations and outlook

High-value mix (cloud + opto) **61%** of H1 sales vs 51% in 2021. Opto NT$7.6bn (19%), IT & consumer NT$16.1bn (40%). Satellite, gaming, and high-end IT PSUs offset a dull PC set.

## What it means for the 800V chain

Do not back-cast 2027 HVDC volume into 2025 numbers. Q2 2025 Lite-On is an AI *power* story, not yet an 800V *architecture* story.
