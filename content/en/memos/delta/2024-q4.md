## Why this company matters for 800 VDC

Delta is one of three Taiwan names on NVIDIA's published 800 VDC ecosystem list, under power system components. It is also the only one whose product map covers nearly the whole stack: server PSUs, power shelves, rack power, BBUs, DC-DC, liquid cooling, and the medium-voltage conversion layer above the rack.

## Key takeaways

- Q4 2024 revenue **NT$114.2bn** (+14% YoY, +2% QoQ), but net income fell to **NT$7.178bn** (-20% YoY, -42% QoQ) and EPS to **NT$2.76**. Gross margin dropped to **30.8%** from 34.9% in Q3.
- Full-year 2024: revenue **NT$421.1bn** (+5%), gross margin **32.4%** (from 29.2%), net income **NT$35.229bn** (+5%), EPS **NT$13.56**. Dividend proposed at **NT$7.00**, just above a 50% payout.
- Q4 margin compression had three causes: an unfavorable mix while AI server products crossed generations, larger inventory write-downs, and a one-off quality problem at the Thailand plant.
- Segment mix in Q4: power electronics **52%**, infrastructure **27%**, automation **12%**, mobility **9%**. Infrastructure grew **21% YoY** on data-center power and cooling.
- Management guided 2025 revenue growth to accelerate, led by AI servers and EV.

## NVIDIA 800V / HVDC

This call is where Delta's ambition moves above the rack.

Asked about **medium-voltage DC** for data centers, management said it is actively developing MV inverters and **solid-state transformers**, and wants to extend from being a power supply vendor into the grid architecture itself. That is precisely the layer NVIDIA's whitepaper reserves for MV rectifiers and SSTs converting 13.8–35 kV AC directly to 800 VDC.

On the rack side, management described the same progression NVIDIA later formalized: GPU power rising from hundreds of watts past 1,500 W, and the power solution evolving from a single **PSU** to an integrated **power shelf** and then to a rack-level **sidecar** or power train. Each step raises Delta's content per rack. DC-DC converters gain importance in the new architecture.

Delta said it would show next-generation Blackwell power and cooling at GTC.

## Operations and outlook

Mobility was flat through 2024 on soft EV demand, but January 2025 revenue alone approached the full prior-year run rate, which management read as a real recovery. Automation returned to growth in Q4 (+7% YoY) and was expected to keep recovering modestly. Capex was guided to stay high.

## What it means for the 800V chain

Delta is the one Taiwan company positioned to sell both ends of NVIDIA's diagram — the facility-level conversion and the in-rack power train. If SSTs become the standard front end, this is the Taiwan name with a stated plan to be there.

**Data caveat:** this is the most recent Delta earnings call in our archive. Delta's 800 VDC products advanced substantially after this date; treat the technology commentary here as the starting point of that story, not its current state.
