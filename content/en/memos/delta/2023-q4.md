## Key takeaways

- FY2023 revenue **NT$401.2bn** (+4%), gross margin **29.2%**, operating margin 10.2%, net income **NT$33.4bn** (+2%), EPS **NT$12.86**. Dividend NT$6.43.
- Q4 2023 revenue **NT$100.0bn** (-5% YoY, -7% QoQ) on soft macro demand, but gross margin improved to **30.4%** and EPS was **NT$3.46**.
- Segment performance for the year: power electronics revenue +9% and profit +12%; automation flat revenue with profit **-53%**; infrastructure revenue -4% with profit **-43%** on weak telecom power.
- Product split of FY2023 revenue: power supplies about **30%**, cooling fans **9–10%**, passives **11–12%**.
- Q4 non-operating income of NT$2.6bn was inflated by converting LELON Electronics convertible bonds into equity — an unrealized valuation gain.

## NVIDIA 800V / HVDC

There is no 800 V discussion on this call, and that absence is the point: in March 2024, **AI server power was roughly 2% of Delta's total revenue**.

What management did flag was the piece that later becomes central to the 800 VDC rack:

- **DC-DC converters** for AI servers were called the bigger opportunity than AC-DC, harder technically, with only about **two companies worldwide** able to mass produce them — Delta being one. Growth of about **100%** was guided for 2024.
- High spec requirements kept competition thin in AI server power generally.

In NVIDIA's eventual architecture the DC-DC stage is exactly where the 64:1 LLC converter sits, taking 800 V to 12 V beside the GPU. Delta was staking that claim before the bus voltage was settled.

## Operations and outlook

EV grew about 80% in 2023 but 2024 guidance was cut from the original 50% growth assumption, while staying profitable on scale. Charging infrastructure grew about 20% and was held back by slow US subsidy disbursement and installation skill requirements for high-power DC chargers. Delta signed a technology license with **Ceres Power** for solid-oxide fuel cells and hydrogen equipment, and named **microgrids** as a further long-term engine. The company kept a five-year double-digit annual growth target.

Automation was being repositioned from machine automation toward process and production automation with more software.

## What it means for the 800V chain

This is the earliest Delta memo in the collection and it sets the base rate: before the 800 VDC era, AI server power was a rounding error at Taiwan's largest power vendor. Everything in the later memos is growth off this base.
