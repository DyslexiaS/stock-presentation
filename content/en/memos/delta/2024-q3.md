## Key takeaways

- Q3 2024 revenue **NT$112.2bn** (+4% YoY, +8% QoQ). Gross margin **34.9%** and operating margin **14.6%** were both record highs. Net income **NT$12.3bn** (+32% YoY), EPS **NT$4.75**.
- Nine-month revenue **NT$306.9bn** (+2%), gross margin **33.0%**, EPS **NT$10.80**.
- Roughly half the margin gain was mix; the other half was accounting — reversals of previously conservative inventory write-downs added **more than 2 percentage points**.
- Segment split for 9M: power electronics **53%** (revenue +7%, profit +26%), infrastructure **23%** (revenue -4%, profit +21%), automation **13%** (revenue -7%, profit -42%), mobility **11%** (revenue +1%, profit -21%).
- Data-center business was about **8%** of company revenue, expected to reach double digits in 2025, with **liquid cooling** named as the single biggest 2025 growth driver.

## NVIDIA 800V / HVDC

The 800 V question was asked directly, and the December 2024 answer is a useful baseline for how fast this architecture moved:

> Will power racks go above 400 V? Not necessarily. 380/400 V is the industrial standard today. To improve energy efficiency, data centers may move toward higher voltages such as **800 V** as a customized standard, to cut current losses and cable cost — but that needs time to develop. For Delta there is no technical problem; it depends on where the market goes.

Ten months later NVIDIA published a whitepaper making 800 VDC the reference architecture. Delta's "no technical problem, depends on the market" framing is what a named ecosystem partner sounds like *before* the standard lands.

What was actually shipping and planned at this point:

- Mainstream PSU at **5.5 kW**, with **8 kW** on the roadmap for the next generation.
- Power architecture centralizing from PSU to **power shelf** to **power rack**, with redundancy rising (GB200 power shelves going from 4–6 to 8).
- **BBUs** as a load buffer for violent GPU swings, sold bundled with the power system; cells bought in, packing partly in-house.
- **Supercapacitors** for higher-frequency noise, complementary to BBU rather than a substitute — but the main supplier's capacity was tiny and volume production was about a year out.

That BBU-plus-supercapacitor split maps directly onto the storage-by-timescale table NVIDIA would later publish.

## Operations and outlook

EV and industrial automation were the drags; management said mobility could even post losses near-term. Inventory rose above NT$80bn at the end of Q3, so Q4 was guided to swing from write-down reversals back to charges, pulling gross margin off the peak. Opex rose 15% on inflation, wages, and the return of travel.

## What it means for the 800V chain

Use this memo as the "before" picture. In late 2024 the money was 5.5 kW PSUs, power shelves, BBUs, and liquid cooling; 800 V was a conditional future. Anyone modeling Delta's 800V revenue in 2024 is modeling something that did not exist yet.
