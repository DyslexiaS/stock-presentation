## Key takeaways

- Q3 2023 revenue **NT$107.8bn** (+1% YoY, +7% QoQ), gross margin **29.6%**, operating margin **11.0%**, net income **NT$9.3bn**, EPS **NT$3.60**.
- Nine-month revenue **NT$301.2bn** (+8%), EPS **NT$9.40**.
- Reorganization announced: from 2024 the three segments become four, with EV powertrain carved out into a new **Mobility** group. EV charging stays in Infrastructure.
- Q3 segment revenue: power electronics **NT$70.4bn** (65% of sales, +11% YoY), automation **NT$13.2bn** (-8% YoY, profit -43%), infrastructure **NT$24.2bn** (-15% YoY, profit -81%) on weak telecom power.
- Capex was NT$21.2bn in nine months and guided to stay **above NT$20bn annually** for several years, with new plants in Taiwan, Thailand, the US, Hungary, India, and Chongqing. R&D held at **8%+ of revenue**.

## NVIDIA 800V / HVDC

No 800 V content — this call predates the architecture entirely. What it does contain is Delta's honest position on AI at the time:

> Hard to estimate revenue contribution, because the AI business model is not yet mature. Hyperscalers are investing aggressively, but whether that converts into sustained shipments is unknown. AI opex is extremely high and very power-hungry; customers need to find a sustainable business model.

Delta was already supplying both **AC/DC and DC/DC** for AI servers plus **air and liquid cooling**, and noted that rising thermal density made cooling a differentiator. But management would not put a number on it.

## Operations and outlook

EV-related revenue was about 12% of 2023 sales with the new Mobility segment targeted at roughly **15% gross margin** — management was explicit that autos are not a high-margin business. Thailand and India together were about **30%** of group capacity, and Delta stated it was *not* exiting China, expecting China to stay above **50%** of capacity.

## What it means for the 800V chain

The value of this memo is chronological. Late 2023: Delta will not forecast AI power. Late 2024: 800 V is a possible custom standard. Early 2025: MV DC and solid-state transformers are on the roadmap. Late 2025: NVIDIA names Delta on the official 800 VDC list. Four calls, one architecture forming in public.
